01

Overview

The DMS supports the nationwide sales operation of ACI Motors across Yamaha motorcycles, tractors, EcoFlow, water pumps, CEAT tyres, lube oil, diesel engines, tools and power tillers. Each business has its own sales tree, field hierarchy, customers, targets and operational rules.

02

Business problem

Field teams collected customer orders across Bangladesh and sent them to head office through WhatsApp. Payment evidence followed the same process: screenshots moved from field staff to business teams and then to finance for approval. The volume of messages and images made approvals, reconciliation, invoicing and operational tracking slow and difficult to audit.

03

My role

I worked on the order, payment, hierarchy and reporting workflows and implemented a major PayLink DMS enhancement. The enhancement brought previously separate business databases and processes into one management platform while preserving each business unit’s individual hierarchy and operational rules.

04

What I designed and implemented

  • Field-force order entry through mobile apps instead of informal WhatsApp messages.
  • Head-office review, discount application and final order approval workflows.
  • Automatic movement of approved orders into an invoice-ready panel.
  • Payment submission with proof and amount, routed directly to finance dashboards for approval.
  • PayLink consolidation for cross-business order and payment management from one platform.
  • Full sales-tree hierarchy tracking, including reporting by supervisor, team and field level.
  • Customer and dealer target-versus-achievement reporting.
  • Credit-customer reporting with mapped credit limits and credit days.
05

Key engineering challenges

Different business models

Nine business lines had separate databases, customers, field hierarchies and operational rules.

Unified without flattening

PayLink needed one control layer while retaining business-specific data boundaries and sales trees.

Financial traceability

Payment evidence, amounts and approval status had to remain visible and auditable across teams.

Hierarchy-aware reporting

Reports needed to explain who sold under whom, target performance and customer credit exposure.

06

Architecture and solution

The solution standardised the order-to-invoice and payment-to-approval journeys while using PayLink as a unified orchestration and reporting layer over business-specific data.

Impact

One governed workflow

Replaced scattered WhatsApp communication with structured, trackable order and payment processes.

Cross-business visibility

Enabled order and payment management across nine business lines from one PayLink platform.

Faster operational handoff

Approved orders automatically became available for invoicing, reducing manual coordination.

Management intelligence
