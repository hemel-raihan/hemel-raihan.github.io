Demand Generation Platform

A self-service demand workflow that replaced giant emailed spreadsheets and manual consolidation with permission-scoped outlet files, high-volume imports and one-click reporting.

01

Overview

Every two days, outlet managers assess which products are running low and submit the quantities their outlet needs. The Demand Generation Platform now manages this cycle digitally across more than 1,000 outlets, from head-office source upload to outlet input and consolidated reporting.

Excel import/exportPermission-scoped outlet dataMaster-detail architectureTemporary history tablesScheduled retentionOne-click consolidation
02

Business problem

Head office maintained a massive spreadsheet containing roughly 150,000 products, columns for around 1,000 outlets and multiple supporting fields such as feedback, allocation and approval. The same file was emailed to every outlet manager, who had to download it, locate the correct outlet and products, enter demand and reply with another file. Head office then manually collected and merged all returned sheets into one report.

03

My role

I designed and developed the Demand Generation solution, including the high-volume data model, upload and export flows, outlet-level permission scoping, temporary-history strategy, scheduled retention and consolidated reporting process.

04

What I designed and implemented

  • Single head-office upload of the large source workbook for each demand cycle.
  • High-volume parsing and insertion of more than one million detail records per cycle.
  • Permission-aware outlet access so managers see and download only their assigned outlet data.
  • Outlet-specific Excel generation instead of distributing the complete national workbook.
  • Manager upload workflow for completed product-demand quantities.
  • One-click consolidation of submitted outlet demand into a head-office report.
  • Temporary table strategy for retaining the previous cycle while the current cycle remains active.
  • Scheduled cleanup of expired data after the one-week retention window.
05

Key engineering challenges

Import scale

Transforming a giant workbook into more than one million normalised detail rows within a dependable cycle.

Outlet isolation

Ensuring each manager can access and modify only the data for permitted outlets.

Cycle continuity

Keeping current demand active while retaining the previous cycle long enough for operational reference.

Fast consolidation

Turning 1,000+ independently submitted outlet files into one usable head-office report.

06

Data architecture and flow

The data model separates cycle metadata, outlets, products and demand values so the system can process large imports and generate outlet-scoped files without repeatedly working against one unstructured spreadsheet.

DemandMasterDemand cycle and source metadata
DemandMasterOutletOutlets included in the cycle
DemandMasterProductProducts available for demand
DemandDetailsOutlet-product demand and workflow values
Cycle retention

Each core table has a corresponding temporary clone. Main tables hold the current active cycle; temporary tables retain previous-cycle records. A scheduler removes expired data after one week.
