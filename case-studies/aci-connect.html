01

Overview

ACI Connect is an enterprise intranet and central application gateway. It gives employees one personalised place to discover company resources, access permitted systems, follow organisational updates and use workplace services.

Nuxt.js employee portalLaravel 12 backendVue 3 adminMicrosoft SQL ServerQueues & schedulerGoogle Maps
02

Business problem

Employees previously depended on fragmented entry points for internal applications and information. Different business units also required distinct access rules, while employee hierarchy and application permissions had to remain aligned with changing HR records.

03

My role

I designed the authentication flow, permission architecture, database structure and core application modules. I also translated stakeholder and operational requirements into scalable workflows across the employee portal and administration dashboard.

04

What I designed and implemented

  • Personalised employee portal with search, social content and a central application launcher.
  • Single sign-on and role-based access control for permitted applications and websites.
  • Automated HR data synchronisation for employee profiles, hierarchy and access decisions.
  • Admin workflows for application access, content, onboarding, knowledge and employee recognition.
  • Visual Google Maps–based office transport route selection.
  • Queued background processing and scheduled data operations.
05

Key engineering challenges

Identity at scale

Keeping employee hierarchy and application permissions consistent across multiple business units.

Distributed data

Working across multiple database connections while maintaining dependable data flows.

Background workloads

Moving synchronisation and recurring processes into queues and scheduled jobs.

Permission clarity

Ensuring each employee sees only the applications and resources relevant to their role.

06

Architecture and solution

The platform separates the employee experience, administrative workflows and core service layer while sharing one permission model and synchronised data foundation.
