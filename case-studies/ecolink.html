01

Overview

Eco-Link manages the product lifecycle after import and sale: SKU-level QR generation, dealer allocation, customer warranty activation and ongoing free or paid service. It gives customers a mobile service channel while providing operational tools for administrators, Technical Service Officers and local electricians.

QR-based activationWarranty lifecycleCustomer mobile appWeb operations dashboardGeo-routed field serviceOTP confirmation
02

Business problem

Renewable-energy customers need a reliable way to verify products, activate warranty and request support after purchase. The business also needs visibility from SKU allocation through service completion, while assigning each request to an appropriate service professional based on customer location.

03

My role

I worked on the connected warranty and service workflow spanning product activation, customer requests, role-specific service operations, assignment logic, paid-service processing and OTP-backed completion. The work required translating customer-service rules into coordinated web and mobile journeys.

04

What I designed and implemented

  • Unique QR generation for each product SKU and allocation to authorised dealers.
  • Customer QR scanning to register a purchased product and activate its warranty.
  • Mobile customer journeys for products, warranty, troubleshooting and service history.
  • Free and paid service-request flows for installation and technical support.
  • Role-specific workflows for customers, Technical Service Officers and local electricians.
  • Address-based routing of requests to the relevant field-service resource.
  • Service-charge handling with customer OTP confirmation before completion.
  • Administrative reporting for request status, payment state and service performance.
05

Key engineering challenges

Product identity

Maintaining a dependable relationship between SKU, QR code, dealer allocation, customer ownership and warranty state.

Location-aware assignment

Routing each request to the correct TSO or local electrician using the customer's service address.

Multi-role workflow

Keeping customer, administrator and field-service actions consistent across web, Android and iOS experiences.

Trusted completion

Preventing unilateral closure by requiring customer OTP confirmation for the completed service and charge.
